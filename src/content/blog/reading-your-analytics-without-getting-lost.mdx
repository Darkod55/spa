## Start from the question

Before opening a report, be clear what decision you are trying to make. That keeps vanity metrics from dominating the conversation.

## Prefer outcome-linked measures

Enquiry quality, referrals and whether people found the right service matter more than raw traffic on its own.

## Review on a rhythm

Regular reviews with context beat one-off deep dives that never change what happens next.
