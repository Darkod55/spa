## Signs it may be time

If your visual identity no longer matches the quality of your services, or different teams are describing the organisation in conflicting ways, a rebrand can create clarity.

## What a rebrand should achieve

A successful rebrand gives people a consistent, recognisable presence across your website, materials and day-to-day communications — not just a new logo.

## Start with strategy

Before design begins, agree who you are speaking to, what you need to be known for, and how the brand will support your priorities.
