## What is changing

People still use traditional search, but more of them are starting conversations with AI tools when they need help. Those tools draw on what they can find publicly — including your website.

## What to do about it

Make sure the essentials are easy to find and clearly written: who you help, what you offer, how to get in touch, and what happens next. That helps both people and the systems summarising your organisation.

## Keep trust first

Visibility without clarity does not help someone in a difficult moment. Build pages that answer real questions honestly, then make sure they can be discovered.
